## Capture System

**My capture intention:** Go wide, and then go deep.

**I'm prioritizing more of:** primary sources, unusual perspectives, long-form that resists summarization, human judgment, first-person experience, emotional resonance.

**I'm cutting back on:** long-form X articles, engagement-optimized content, anything being pushed by an algorithm.
