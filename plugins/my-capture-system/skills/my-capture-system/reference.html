2AI-Era Shifts

More of
  • Primary sources
  • Unusual perspectives
  • Long-form that resists summarization
  • Human judgment
  • First-person experience
  • Emotional resonance
Less of
  • Long-form X articles
  • Engagement-optimized junk
  • Anything being pushed by an algorithm
Why

Unique outputs require unique inputs. My consumption diet is my moat.
