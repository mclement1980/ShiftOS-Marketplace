# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report a suspected vulnerability privately to KHM Shift through the contact information in your ShiftOS program materials. Include the affected version, platform route, reproduction steps, and the minimum evidence needed to understand the issue.

Do not include credentials, private organizational material, or sensitive deliverables in the report. Replace them with a minimal safe reproduction whenever possible.

## Supported release

Security fixes target the current ShiftOS-DISPATCH release. KHM Shift may ask a reporter to verify a fix in a controlled local copy before a new release is published.

## Scope

The packages provide guided local workflow instructions. Platform security, account access, and runtime permissions remain governed by Claude or Codex and by the participant's organization.
