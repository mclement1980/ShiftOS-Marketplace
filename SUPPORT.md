# Support

ShiftOS cohort participants should use their cohort's support channel or the KHM Shift contact information in their program welcome materials.

When requesting help, include:

- whether you are using Claude or Codex;
- whether you installed from the repository marketplace or a ZIP;
- the ShiftOS-DISPATCH version;
- the exact command or step that failed; and
- a redacted error message that contains no credentials or private organizational data.

Installation support establishes that the companion is available. It does not certify completion of the exercise, quality of the deliverable, or transfer into workplace practice.
